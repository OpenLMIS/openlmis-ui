https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-203 · Parent: [FM-199] Guided onboarding tours for new users

Stacked on FM-202 (PR #150), which carries the module's loading and navigation waits.

## Scope

- **In:**
  - a tour for the Transaction History list;
  - a tour for Transaction Details;
  - `data-tour` anchors on both screens.
- **One shared change:** `CardListSkeleton` gets an outer `aria-busy` div, so the runner also waits

- **Copy fixes to earlier tours, found on phones:**
  - requisition detail: the info icon is in table headers only; Find Products names View;
  - Stock Card and Stock On Hand Print name the chosen packs or doses.
- **Out:**
  - the Reverse editor, a form page whose tour is a separate decision (follow-up note on FM-199);
  - onboarding module changes;
  - anchors in shared components (the picker, breadcrumbs, `data-table/`);
  - per-row View buttons, which repeat.

## Summary

Transaction History and Transaction Details get a Show Guide button.

The Transaction History guide walks through:
- choosing a facility and program, then Search;
- finding a transaction by document number;
- filtering by type and dates;
- choosing columns;
- the results, where View opens a transaction.

The Transaction Details guide walks through:
- Reverse (when the user may reverse it) and Print;
- the transaction summary;
- the Packs/Doses switch and View;
- the lines, with links to the transactions that reverse them.

Before Search, the list guide shows only what is on screen.

Legacy has the same screens, with Document Number inside Filter; the new UI shows it as the search
box.

Content intent:
1. Choose My Facility or Supervised Facility, then the program (and, for Supervised Facility, the
   facility), and press Search.
2. After Search, find a transaction by its document number (legacy kept it inside Filter).

4. Choose the columns or card details to show.
5. Neutral: transactions appear here. View (an eye icon on narrow screens) opens one, and the pager moves
   between pages. It must read true over the skeleton, the date-range message and errors.

Content intent:
1. Reverse opens a form to undo some or all lines.
2. Print the transaction as a PDF, in the chosen packs or doses.
3. The document number, type, date, who performed it and the signature.
4. Show quantities in packs or doses.
5. Choose the columns or card details to show.
6. One line per product or lot; Reversed By (on a reversed line) and Reversing (on a reversal) link
   to the related transaction, and the pager moves between pages.

### Rules

- **Buttons in the header actions:** Reverse and Print carry the anchor on the `Button` itself.
  `WorkspaceActions` stretches its direct children (`*:flex-1`), so a wrapper would take the stretch.
- **Anchors outside the boundaries:** the results and lines anchors sit outside their
  `QueryBoundary`, because the card-layout skeletons are not `aria-busy`. An anchor inside them
  would be missing while cards load, and the step would be dropped.
- **No anchors on the details pending view.** The list's toolbar anchors also render inside the busy
  list skeleton; the runner waits for it, as on Stock On Hand.
- **Reverse and Print stay disabled until the lines load.** With `CardListSkeleton` busy, the runner
  waits for the lines in both layouts, so the tour starts on enabled buttons. An event with no lines
  keeps them disabled; the steps still name them.
- **Labels:**
  - In es and fr the columns menu and the row button use different words (Vista/Ver,
    Affichage/Voir); the copy uses the right one per step.
  - Reverse is "Annuler" in fr.

## UI/UX

- **Improve:** legacy explains nothing. The guide names the order of work and where Reverse and Print
  are.
- **Drop:** nothing.
- **Phone:** the same steps against the card layout.
- **Arabic:** placements mirror through the module.

## Decisions

- **Two tours:** details is its own route. Reverse is a form and stays without a tour here.
- **Search inside the picker step and View explained in the results step:** as in FM-202.
- **No new module changes:** FM-202's waits cover these screens once card skeletons are busy too.
- **Busy card skeletons through a plain outer div:** `CardList busy` would also dim the skeleton.
- **No offline step:** Transaction History is not an offline page.
