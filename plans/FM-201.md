# FM-201: Onboarding Tour: App Shell (Home)

https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-201 · Parent: [FM-199] Guided onboarding tours for new users
(React Joyride, isolated module)

Stacked on FM-200 (PR #148), which ships the onboarding module.

## Scope

- **In:**
  - a shell tour on Home, opening with the Home dashboard;
  - `data-tour` anchors on the shell;
  - module changes:
    - steps whose part of the page exists but is hidden are left out;
    - `TourStep.expand` and `spotlight`, which open, close and give back menu sections;
    - Ctrl/Cmd+B is blocked while a tour runs.
- **Out:**
  - single dashboard widgets, which vary by right;
  - the account menu's contents, which are portalled and only exist while open;
  - other screens.

## Summary

People coming from legacy know a blue menu bar, a visible Log Out button and a language
dropdown. The new UI moves these into a side menu, an account menu and the menu's footer.

On Home, Show Guide now starts with the dashboard, then walks through the parts of the app
around every page:
- the menu and how to hide it;
- search;
- the account menu, where Log Out lives;
- the connection notices and the language switch;
- the Show Guide button itself.

On a phone the menu is closed, so the guide skips what is not on screen.

## Agent Brief

### Module Fix

`presentSteps` keeps a step only when its anchor exists **and** `checkVisibility()` holds.
Browsers without the method (Safari before 17.4, still in Vite's default target) fall back to
`getClientRects()`, so hidden parts are still left out there. Without this:
- the hidden palette button below 640px still counts in "Step N of M";
- the page stays dimmed for about 1 s before Joyride skips it.

### Steps (`tours/home.tour.ts`)

| # | Anchor | Placed on | Expanded | Collapsed | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `home-dashboard` | root div of `HomeDashboard`, `home-dashboard.tsx`, including its empty state | shown | shown | shown |
| 1 | `shell-navigation` | `SidebarContent`, `app-sidebar.tsx` | list | icon rail | left out (sheet closed) |
| 1a | `shell-section:<nav key>`, one per section; spotlight on `shell-navigation` | section item (`Collapsible` or flyout item), `app-sidebar.tsx` | opened for its step, closed after | icon only | left out |
| 2 | `shell-menu-toggle` | `SidebarTrigger` in `custom-trigger.tsx`, so whichever toggle is mounted | sidebar header | top bar | top bar |
| 3 | `shell-search` | palette button, `command-palette.tsx` | shown | shown | left out (hidden below 640px) |
| 4 | `shell-account` | avatar button, `app-header.tsx` | shown | shown | shown |

| 6 | `shell-guide` | Show Guide button, `tour-launcher.tsx` | shown | shown | shown |

Placements:
- step 0 uses `center`: the dashboard is taller than the screen, so a `top` tooltip ran off it;
- steps 1, 1a (every section) and 5 use `inline-end`, mapped to `left` or `right` by direction;
- the rest use `bottom`.

### Menu Sections

- Steps for BUQ, CCE, Orders, Reports, Requisitions, Stock Management and Administration use the
  nav label as title and `onboarding.home.section.<name>` as content. Sections the user cannot
  see (rights, no live pages) are left out by `presentSteps`.
- `TourStep.expand` gives the step Joyride `before`/`after` hooks in the runner: open the
  section's `collapsible-trigger` if closed, close it after the step, and close every section
  step's section when the tour ends. The menu shows one section at a time, so the runner remembers the section
  the user had open and reopens it when the tour ends, even when the tour ends on that section's
  own step. If the tour ends because the page changed, the runner leaves the menu alone, so the new
  page's own section stays open.
- Section steps anchor the tooltip on the section's button, so the arrow points at its name and
  not the middle of an open list. The collapsed rail has no collapsible
  trigger, so it only spotlights the icon.
- `TourStep.spotlight` highlights the whole menu for section steps, so a tall section such as
  Administration never spills the spotlight over the footer.
- Ctrl/Cmd+B is blocked while a tour runs; collapsing mid-step would replace an opened section by
  its icon and leave it open. The menu toggle step says the shortcut works once the guide is closed.

### Rules

- Do not anchor:
  - `Sidebar` itself, whose props are lost on the mobile `Sheet`;
  - group labels, which are `opacity-0` when collapsed;
  - the empty notices div.
- Step 2's text covers the phone case ("open the menu here"). Step 4 names Log Out for legacy
  users.

### Risks

- **Opening the mobile sheet:** it is a modal, so it ends the tour. That is intended, as in FM-200.
- **Notices vary:** they come and go (downloading, offline, none). The footer anchor covers them,
  because the theme and language row is always there.

## UI/UX

- **Improve:**
  - the menu bar becomes a side menu that can collapse;
  - search by page name replaces hunting through hover menus;
  - one account menu holds Log Out.
- **Drop:** nothing; the guide only explains.
- **Phone:** four steps (dashboard, menu toggle, account, Show Guide).
- **Expanded and collapsed sidebar:** 13 steps for an administrator (six sections).

## Decisions

- **Containers, not menu items:** anchors go on containers that exist in every state, so loading
  and rights changes do not break steps.
- **No step inside the account menu:** its content exists only while open, and opening it would end
  the tour.
- **Phone:** hidden parts are left out rather than redirected, keeping the tour data-only. Step 2
  explains how to open the menu and where the language switch is.
- **Opening sections from the module:** the runner clicks the section's
  `[data-slot="collapsible-trigger"]` and reads `aria-expanded`. The shell stays attribute-only
  (AC 5), but section steps depend on the shadcn slot name: if `shadcn add` renames it, sections
  stop opening while the steps still play.
- **Back To Legacy in the copy:** the navigation, account and footer steps name it, because
  screens not yet migrated (BUQ, among others) are only in the old version.
