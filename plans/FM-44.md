# FM-44: Requisition: Read-Only View

https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-44 · Parent: [FM-39] Requisitions

Shipped in one PR with [FM-48](FM-48.md), branch `feat/fm-48-44-view-requisitions`. This first
requisition slice also decides the grid architecture and the performance budget the FM-56 spike
was meant to decide (the spike is dropped; see [FM-39](FM-39.md) Decisions).

## Scope

- Requisition page `/requisitions/view/$requisitionId?tab=` with header, comments, tabs (Full
  Supply, Non Full Supply, Patients), template-driven grid with category groups, client paging,
  totals and Print. Every record opens read-only in this slice.
- Foundations [FM-41] to [FM-43] build on: template compiler, line model, display calculations,
  capability policy, and a read-only grid fed by compiled columns for both table and cards.
- Not in scope: any editing or action (Sync, Submit, Authorize, Approve, Reject, Delete, Skip, Add
  Product, Add Comment); offline caching and the outdated-offline banner, which wait for the
  requisition offline pass ([FM-39] step 12). What this slice does offline is under Offline.

## Summary

A requisition is a facility's report and order for one period. Anyone allowed to view it can open
it to see what the facility reported, what it asked for, what was approved, the comments and the
total cost, and can print it.

Legacy shows one screen for editing and viewing, with everything disabled when the user may not
change it. It downloads about 10 MB of product data at sign-in to show product names, and
recalculates many figures in the browser. Users who may only view requisitions cannot open them in
legacy at all, even though the server allows it.

The new page opens any requisition the server lets the user see, loads only that requisition's own
product versions, and shows the header, comments, tabs, grid and totals with Print. Until the
editing stories ship, every requisition opens read-only, including ones the user could edit in
legacy, where editing keeps working meanwhile. Phones get readable product cards instead of a
sideways-scrolling spreadsheet.

## Legacy Today

Routes `requisitions.requisition.fullSupply`, `.nonFullSupply`, `.patients`

- **Heading** "Report and Requisition for <program> (<facility type>)" and Print.

  when none); District (the facility's own zone) and Region (its parent), whatever their levels;
  Reporting Period as dates only; Date Physical Stock Count Completed when set. No status.
- **Comments**: the latest status message (status, author, "on", date, text) and View History, a
  "Requisition History" dialog of every message, newest first. Read-only shows no Add Comment and

- **Tabs**: Patient(s) when the template enables it, which also renames Full Supply to "Priority
  drugs and medical supplies" and Non Full Supply to "Additional drugs and medical supplies";
  Full supply product(s); Non full supply product(s) when the program shows that tab.
- **Grid**: template columns by `displayOrder`, labels and definitions (header tooltips) from the
  template; on stock-based templates the Losses and Adjustments definition adds "Total losses and
  adjustments modal is disabled for stock based requisitions."; Pack Size after the product name.
  Non-full and emergency grids use eight columns. Approved Quantity and Remarks from Authorized
  onwards. Category heading rows on each page of ten. Skipped rows dimmed. Losses and adjustments
  open a dialog (Reason, Quantity, Total, "There are no adjustments"). Packs/Doses toggle converts
  the quantity columns only. Read-only hides the product filter and skip controls. Empty tabs:
  "No full supply items have been added." / "No non-full supply items have been added."

  Facility: <names>", "(stock unavailable)" or "not configured", an access-denied banner, a column
  following Packs/Doses, shortfall highlight when approved quantity exceeds it.
- **Totals**: Total Requisition Cost with Show Details (full supply subtotal; the non-full subtotal
  never shows, a defect), money formatted from `/localeSettings`.
- **Calculated values** are recomputed in the browser on every load, even read-only.
- **Patients**: Skip and Month Of Treatment columns, then months 1 to 12; TB (five rows) and
  Leprosy (four rows) groups; each row's skip state and enabled months come from the stored
  `patientsData`.
- **Print** opens `/api/requisitions/{id}/print?showInDoses=&lang=&access_token=` in a new tab.

Observed: srmanager2 AUTHORIZED, srmanager4 APPROVED, RELEASED and SKIPPED, psupervisor SUBMITTED
are read-only with Print; srmanager4 also sees Delete on SKIPPED. Timing (headless, cold):

change 159 to 253 ms; tab switch 128 ms.

### Grid Architecture

The grid lives in `src/features/requisitions/` (batch approval will sit in the same feature). It
moves to `src/components/editable-grid/` only when Stock adopts it. Physical Inventory is the
pattern, not a dependency: its grid has a fixed column tuple, reads its editor's form store and
imports stock-events code. Shared pieces reused: `useCardView` (layout and stored
View choices; no measured fit, since every template column stays and the table scrolls),
`TableCards` and the `CardList` parts, `usePageStart`, the headless `useTable` pager.
`TableRow`, `TableCards` and `CardListItem` gain per-row `tone` (`skipped` uses grey with full-strength text, `shortfall` warns with a labelled icon); Skip, product code and name stay pinned at the inline start, as legacy, with matching opaque surfaces, their own divider lines in place of collapsed borders and a Total cell spanning them; offsets come from header widths measured by ResizeObserver.

1. **Template compiler** `lib/template.ts`: `compileColumns(template, context)` turns `columnsMap`
   into ordered `GridColumn`s keyed by `name` (never indicator; `skipped` and
   `calculatedOrderQuantityIsa` both use S). Context: tab, emergency, status and supplying stock.
   Each column: name, label, definition (plus the stock-based Losses And Adjustments note), kind (`product`, `quantity`, `count`,
   `currency`, `text`, `skip`, `adjustments`, `unit`, `packSize`, `supplyingStock`),
   `convertsToPacks` (legacy's list: beginning balance, received, consumed, stock on hand,
   approved, maximum stock, average and adjusted consumption, requested, calculated order
   quantity, TE, TF, TG, losses and adjustments), `sticky`. Rules:
   - `isDisplayed`, by `displayOrder`; Pack Size after `orderable.fullProductName`.
   - `packsToShip` options `showPackToShipInAllPages` / `showPackToShipInApprovalPage`.
   - Approval columns (`approvedQuantity`, `remarks`; `totalReceivedQuantity` and `remarks` on
     patients templates) from Authorized onwards, Released Without Order included.
   - Non-full and emergency grids: requested quantity, explanation, code, name, unit
     (`orderable.dispensable.displayUnit`), packs to ship, approved quantity, remarks.
   - `supplyingFacilityStockOnHand` after the highest displayed column before its order, when the
     template shows it and the policy predicts approval, as legacy.
2. **Line model** `lib/lines.ts`: joins each line with its product version, its approved product
   and its program entry, taking the **last** entry for the program as legacy does (one Essential
   Meds product lists the program twice with different prices); splits full and non-full by that
   entry's `fullSupply`; sorts by category order, category name, product order, product name
   (non-full too, where legacy kept server order); hides skipped lines under
   `hideSkippedLineItems`; filters by product name or code.
3. **Display values** `lib/calculations.ts` (below).
4. **Rendering**: one `GridColumn[]` feeds one `useTable`, rendered by `RequisitionGrid` (table at
   or above 672 px) and inline `TableCards` (below). The table follows `PhysicalInventoryGrid`:
   `DataTableCard`, `Table density="compact" layout="auto"`, category rows as
   `TableRow surface="primary"` spanning every column, headers in `RequisitionGridHeader`: labels wrap between words up to three lines, as legacy, so columns stay narrow (an exception to the one-line header rule), and a template definition shows as an info icon button with a Tooltip. The cards
   are `TableCards` with the product column as `title` and `heading` for category rows, which
   renders the same heading and card markup as Physical Inventory's cards. Cell renderers by
   `kind`, hook-free so `TableCards` can call them; [FM-41] adds editable renderers behind the
   capability policy. Skipped rows use grey with full-strength text; shortfalls have a labelled
   warning icon and tint. A column-aligned totals footer sits under the table when its cost column
   is visible; the Total section follows every tab, including Patients. Client paging with `detailPage`,
   `detailSize` (default 10). The table scrolls sideways when the template's columns do not fit;
   nothing is truncated.

### Display Calculations

Legacy recomputes calculated columns on every load, and the test server's seed records store few
of them (the Released Family Planning record stores no packs or cost; its $25,434.54 exists only as
a browser calculation). Stored values can also be stale: 42e6e295 stores a $3,141.30 total that its
own inputs give as $0.00, which is what legacy shows. So the page computes, from stored inputs:

- Full-supply, non-skipped lines of regular requisitions: D, E, C, Y, N, P, H, I, S in that order,
  each only when the template's source is calculated, with the server's formulas
  (`RequisitionLineItem.java:621-808`). D comes first, from `stockAdjustments` and the record's
  reason snapshot; on stock-based templates D is shown as stored. With the server's formulas ([FM-39] Calculation Rules), as [FM-39] decided ("where legacy's browser
  differs, the server wins"): N is 0 when C is 0 and rounds the ratio as the server; P is
  `ceil(N/2)` with `enableAvgConsumptionForCurrentPeriod`; H rounds half up.
- Every non-skipped line: packs to ship and cost, with the server's order quantity: in Initiated,
  Rejected and Submitted, requested quantity when not null, else ISA quantity on stock-based
  templates or calculated quantity on others, else 0; in every other status approved quantity, or
  0 when null.
- Patients: TC from this record's `patientsData` when its source is calculated, except that in
  Authorized, In Approval and Approved a stored TC that differs wins (an approver's override, as
  legacy `calculation.factory.js:110-138`); TE to TH follow whichever TC is shown.
- Inputs (`USER_INPUT`, `STOCK_CARDS`, `REFERENCE_DATA`, `PREVIOUS_REQUISITION`) shown as stored;
  price per pack and doses per patient from the pinned version's program entry.
- Totals sum non-skipped lines' cost, full and non-full separately, even when the cost and packs
  columns are hidden.
- Packs display with `cardQuantity` (`src/lib/quantity.ts`), which already matches legacy (toward
  zero, signed remainder, 0 without a pack size).

Program entry exception: the server takes the first matching program entry from an unordered set
(`OrderableDto.java:67-73`), so the page deliberately follows legacy's last entry instead; this is
the one place display values follow legacy rather than the server.

On the recorded test records this gives legacy's figures ($723.50, $0.00, $25,434.54, $6,673.92).
It differs from legacy only where legacy's browser formulas differ from the server's: requested
quantity filled but its column hidden, adjusted consumption at C=0 with Z, the current-period
average, half-up maximum stock, and Released Without Order priced from approved quantity.

### Capability Policy

`lib/capabilities.ts`: `requisitionCapabilities({ status, emergency, template, program, grants,
approvalNodes, currentNodeId, unskipWhenApproving, isSplit })`. `grants` are the user's rights at
the record's facility and program; `approvalNodes` are the supervisory nodes where the user holds a
role with APPROVE for the program (from role assignments); `currentNodeId` is the record's
`supervisoryNode`. The server assigns that node when a requisition is authorized
(`BaseRequisitionController.java:625-632`), so AUTHORIZED and IN_APPROVAL records carry it and the
policy compares it directly. Only when it is null does the route resolve it as the server does
(`RoleAssignmentPermissionValidator.java:94`): the user's APPROVE nodes through
`supervisoryNodesByIdsOptions`, then each node's `requisitionGroupOptions`, keeping nodes whose
member facilities include the record's facility. These are the same lookups [FM-48] uses for
partner facilities. Legacy reads every requisition group for this (`permission.service.js:272-330`);
`unskipWhenApproving` is the `GET requisitions/unSkipRequisition` setting, read by [FM-43], false
until then. Pure, mirrors legacy `requisition-view.factory.js`, `requisition.js:493-519` and
`requisition-view-tab.controller.js:518-537`:

| Output | Rule |
|---|---|
| `editable` and `editableColumns` | user-input columns for CREATE on INITIATED/REJECTED, AUTHORIZE on SUBMITTED; approved quantity and remarks (plus TB Monthly received and TC) for APPROVE on AUTHORIZED/IN_APPROVAL; none otherwise |
| `actions` | sync, submit (or submit and authorize with `skipAuthorization`), authorize, approve, reject (not split), delete (DELETE with CREATE on INITIATED/REJECTED/SKIPPED, with AUTHORIZE on SUBMITTED), skip (regular, INITIATED/REJECTED, `periodsSkippable`, skip column shown), print |
| `comments` | follows `editable` |
| `addFullSupplyProduct` | creator or authorizer editing an emergency requisition, or unskipping hidden skipped lines on a regular one |
| `addNonFullSupplyProduct` | creator or authorizer editing, program shows the non-full tab |
| `skipLines` | creator or authorizer editing a regular requisition with the Skip column; approvers only with `unskipWhenApproving` |
| `approver` | APPROVE at the record's node on AUTHORIZED/IN_APPROVAL (supplying stock column) |
| `deferred` | actions the policy allows that the shipped gate hides (for the legacy link) |

The shipped action gate includes Sync, Submit, Submit and Authorize, Authorize, Skip, Delete and
Print. Creator and authorizer editing now ships with [FM-41]; approver and Patients editing
remain gated for their own stories. Approval depends on the current supervisory node on the
server; the policy predicts from grants, as legacy, and the server stays authoritative.

### Rules

- VIEW-only users open requisitions read-only ([FM-39] decision); the server's 403 decides.
- Key columns by name; render template labels and definitions as data.
- Patients: values, skip state and enabled months only from this record's `patientsData`, never
  local storage; read-only never rewrites it; defaults (TB rows months 1 to 6, Leprosy PB rows 1 to
  12, MB rows 1 to 6) only when `patientsData` is empty.
- Comments and history from `statusMessages`, sorted newest first by `createdDate`; authors shown
  when the server sends names. Rejection reasons from the latest REJECTED `statusHistory` entry by
  `createdDate`, using `rejectionDtos` and `statusMessageDto` for the author and date.
  History and rejection actions show only when their respective lists contain entries.
- Adjustments dialog: reasons from the record's snapshot, matched by `id`, including named
  existing hidden reasons; on stock-based templates the total is plain text with no dialog.
- Empty values per AGENTS.md (blank in tables through `tableValue`, `EMPTY_VALUE` in card rows),
  never 0.
- Read-only: no writes, so no `useSessionMutation`; Print uses `usePrintReport`'s own guards.
  Feature code reads identity only through `src/lib/session-scope.ts`; the route passes `userId`.

### Performance Budget

Method: a production build (`pnpm build` then `pnpm preview`, as the service worker section of
AGENTS.md) in headless Chrome, signed in, with the time zone, locale settings and permissions
already cached (warm session) and the record not cached. Each measure runs five times and the
median is recorded, with `performance.mark` at navigation start and the first painted grid row
(`requestAnimationFrame` after the row mounts). Real records: 790da33b and ddfa5b0c against test.
Synthetic record: 500 lines with every column, served by fulfilling `GET v2/requisitions/{id}`,
period, facility, program and status messages, and the two hydration `POST` searches, locally from
fixture JSON (nothing reaches the server). Desktop 1440 px and phone 390 px:

### Risks And Edge Cases

- `orderablesByIdentitiesOptions` keeps one version per product id; a requisition holds one

- Author names arrive null on test; no user lookups (they need `USERS_MANAGE`).
- No TB Monthly requisition on test; the Patients tab is checked on a stubbed record unless a

- Records editable in legacy open read-only until [FM-41]; srmanager4 loses Delete on SKIPPED

## Decisions

- Header facts and comments load independently of approval policy lookups. The legacy note and
  tabs each isolate their policy lookups in their own boundary.
- Tabs calculate the line models and totals once and pass them to the active grid.
- Time zone and currency share the cached `currencySettingsOptions` request. The time zone keeps
  its own derived query for the stored-zone fallback and retry behavior.
- Once the blocking loader returns the record, pending grid columns come from its template and
  the toolbar uses the same disabled controls as the loaded grid. Pending tabs stack at phone
  widths; the heading skeleton is shared across route and section loading states.
- Packs/Doses and View form the trailing toolbar group, stretching on narrow screens as Stock.
- A grid without a visible cost column omits the table footer and uses `RequisitionTotals` below.
  Physical Inventory likewise summarizes values in their visible columns; no empty summary row
  is added solely for a Total label.

- Every record opens read-only until [FM-41]; the policy computes legacy's capabilities and the
  shipped gate shows only Print.
- Display values are computed from stored inputs with the server's formulas, as [FM-39] decided.
- Program entry: the last one for the program, as legacy.
- Grid lives in the feature; it moves to `src/components/editable-grid/` when Stock adopts it.
- Money formats from `/localeSettings` currency fields, the deployment's configured currency.
- Requisition VIEW access counts supervision roles ([FM-48] Rights); Print trusts the server once
  the record has loaded.
- The performance budget and virtualization rule above replace the FM-56 spike and [FM-27]'s
  budget.
- **Legacy link** (2026-10-27): until [FM-41], a record whose `deferred` list is not empty
  (editing, Approve, Delete on SKIPPED and so on) shows the note under UI/UX. It reuses Back To
  Legacy: `useBackToLegacy` takes an optional hash, appended to `legacyHome(BASE_URL)`, and
  `onClick` keeps `whenLeaveAllowed(..., 'leave')` and `allowUnload()`. When `available` is false
  (no URL prefix, as in dev) there is no legacy UI to open, so the note is hidden, as Back To
  Legacy.
- **Delete on Skipped** (2026-10-27) waits for [FM-54]; the legacy link covers it, since
  `deferred` includes Delete.
- **District and Region** (2026-10-27): legacy's labels whatever the zone levels, so Lilongwe
  Health Center shows "District: Lilongwe, Region: Lilongwe District".
- **Patients tab** (2026-10-27) is checked on a stubbed TB record.
- Requisition screens follow the Stock Management design and reuse its components (2026-10-27);
  see Stock Consistency.

## Offline

Cold opens require the server; already-open detail pages retain editing offline:

- `registerOpenOfflinePages` retains open requisition details for the same session. Edits remain
  available and autosave on the device. Cold offline opens show Not Available Offline.
- Print disables offline through `usePrintReport`'s `offline` with `offline.print-description`,
  as Stock Card.
- Load failures go only through `ErrorFallback`, `ListError` and `QueryBoundary`, which use
  `useOfflineFailure`. Device drafts use Dexie; there is no download provider, `deviceQuery` or
  `SnapshotAlert`.
