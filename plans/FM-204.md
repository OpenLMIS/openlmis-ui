## Scope

- **In:**
  - a tour for the Physical Inventory program list;
  - a tour for the draft editor;
  - `data-tour` anchors on both screens.
- **Out:**
  - onboarding module changes;
  - anchors in shared components: the stock program picker (shared with Issue, Receive, Adjustments
    and Unpack), `data-table/` and the workspace footer;
  - per-row parts: status badges, Start/Continue, per-line inputs and Reasons buttons;
  - the dialogs (Add Products, Reasons, Occurred Date, Print), which explain themselves;
  - barcode scanning, which sits behind a flag.

## Summary

Physical Inventory gets a Show Guide button on both screens.

The program list guide explains, in one step, that each program at the home facility shows whether a
count is under way:
- Start begins a new count;
- Continue opens the draft;
- offline, only drafts saved on this device open.

The draft guide walks through:
- the progress bar;
- Filters (Keywords and Include Inactive Items);
- the saved-on-this-device status, and how it differs from Save;
- Packs/Doses and View;
- Add Product;
- the lines in two steps:
  - enter Current Stock, with VVM where it applies;
  - add Reasons for any difference until Unaccounted Quantity is 0;
- Delete;
- Save and Submit, with the date and signature asked on Submit.

Legacy has the same screens and explains nothing. Its users stumble on these, and the guide covers each:
- Unaccounted Quantity's sign;
- why Submit fails;
- the difference between autosave and Save.

## Legacy Today

- **Program list** (`#!/stockmanagement/physicalInventory`):
  - home facility only;
  - Program, Status (Not yet started / Draft) and Start/Continue.
- **Draft page:**
  - toolbar: progress ("x of y products completed"), Filter (Keywords, Include inactive items),
    "All changes are saved", Add Product, Packs/Doses;
  - lines grouped by category: Stock on Hand, Current Stock, VVM Status (only when a product uses
    VVM), Reasons, Unaccounted Quantity;
  - sticky footer: Delete, Save, Submit.
- **Submit:** validates every line, asks Occurred date (required, not in the future) and an optional
  signature, then offers Print and opens Stock on Hand.
- **Unaccounted Quantity:** (Current Stock - Stock on Hand) - reasons; it must be 0.
- **Screenshots:** `.screenshots/FM-204/legacy-*.png`.

## Agent Brief

### Steps: Program List (`tours/physical-inventory.tour.ts`)

Route id `/(protected)/_protected/stock-management/physical-inventory`.

| # | Key | Anchor | Placed on | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `programs` | `physical-inventory-programs` | new plain div around the single `StockProgramPicker` in the route's `ProgramsTable`; `ListError` stays outside | bottom |

Content intent: programs you can count at your home facility appear here, with the status of each count.
Start begins a new count, Continue opens your draft, and Try Again reloads a status that could not be
read. Offline, only drafts saved on this device can be opened. It must read true over the empty states
(no home facility, no programs) the wrapper also covers.

### Steps: Draft Editor (`tours/physical-inventory-draft.tour.ts`)

Route id `/(protected)/_protected/stock-management/physical-inventory_/$programId`.

| # | Key | Anchor | Placed on | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `progress` | `physical-inventory-progress` | progress div, `physical-inventory-pending.tsx` `InventoryToolbar` | bottom |
| 2 | `filters` | `physical-inventory-filters` | Filters div, same toolbar | bottom |
| 3 | `saved` | `physical-inventory-saved` | draft status div, same toolbar | bottom |
| 4 | `unit` | `physical-inventory-unit` | unit toggle div (only when units can switch) | bottom |
| 5 | `view` | `physical-inventory-view` | View div | bottom |
| 6 | `add` | `physical-inventory-add` | Add Product div | bottom |
| 7 | `lines` | `physical-inventory-add`, spotlight `physical-inventory-lines` (new plain div around the grid-or-empty ternary and its pager, `physical-inventory-editor.tsx`) | as 6 and the wrapper | bottom |
| 8 | `reasons` | as 7 | as 7 | bottom |
| 9 | `delete` | `physical-inventory-delete` | Delete `Button` directly, `inventory-actions.tsx` | top |
| 10 | `actions` | `physical-inventory-actions` | Save/Submit group div, `inventory-actions.tsx` | top |

Content intent:
1. A product is complete once each of its lots has a Current Stock; the count follows your filters.
2. Keywords finds lines by product code or name, lot, expiry, stock on hand or count. Include Inactive
   Items shows inactive lines with no stock.
3. Changes save on this device as you type, so you can leave or work offline. This is not Save, which
   stores the draft on the server for others to see.
4. Show and enter quantities in packs or doses.
5. Choose the columns or card details to show.
6. Add a product or lot not listed yet, with its current stock. If you may manage lots, you can also
   add a new lot.
7. Neutral over an empty list: each line is a product or lot; enter the Current Stock you counted, 0 if
   there is none. Stock On Hand is what the system expects. VVM Status appears for products that use
   it. A line just added can be removed and one with no stock deactivated, each with its own button
   (a column that shows only when needed, or the card band).
8. Neutral over an empty list: Add Reasons works once Current Stock is entered. On a counted line,
   negative Unaccounted Quantity means stock is missing (add a reason that removes stock), positive
   means extra; an uncounted line shows minus its stock on hand. It must be 0 on every line before
   you can submit.
9. Delete removes this draft after you confirm and returns to the program list. It needs a connection.
10. Save stores the draft on the server; newly added lots can no longer be edited after it. Submit
    needs every line counted and Unaccounted Quantity at 0, with inactive lines hidden, and takes you
    to any line to fix. It then asks for the date of the count and an optional signature. Save and
    Submit need a connection. Print and the move to Stock On Hand are left out: the user sees them
    right after.

### Rules

- **No anchors on the loading toolbar:**
  - `InventoryToolbar` also renders, disabled, in the busy pending body.
  - It sets its anchors only when not `disabled`, so a load longer than the runner's 20 s wait gives
    up instead of playing a partial tour on placeholders that are then replaced under an open step.
- **Lines steps hang from Add Product:** the grid starts right under the toolbar and fills the screen,
  so a `top` tooltip ran off the top at 1440. `center` fits but Joyride then drops the spotlight. The
  tooltip targets Add Product and the spotlight stays on the lines (`spotlight`, as Home's sections).
- **Lines anchor outside the grid internals:**
  - Wrap the `filtered.length ? grid : empty` ternary, so no-matches still has the anchor.
  - The editor's focus query (`region.querySelector`) still finds the table or list.
- **Footer anchors inside the feature:**
  - `WorkspaceFooterPortal` is shared, so the anchors go on the feature's own Delete `Button` and
    Save/Submit div.
  - The Delete anchor passes through `ActionTooltip`'s render.
- **States with no anchors:**
  - These show only an empty state: no draft, a missing device copy offline, no view right, and a load error.
  - The runner gives up after 5 s, as on other screens.
- **Disabled buttons:**
  - Delete and Save are disabled offline. Submit stays enabled and explains that it needs a connection.
  - Save and Submit stay disabled while the eligible products load (`writeDisabled`,
    `eligibleFetching`); that load is not `aria-busy`, so the tour can start on them disabled.
  - The steps still name them.
- **Late changes under an open step:**
  - The list's per-row statuses load as plain skeletons, not `aria-busy`, so rows can read Start, then
    Continue.
  - The VVM column can appear once eligible products load, widening the table.
  - The draft status can grow to a failure sentence with Retry.
  - At narrow widths the status div is `basis-full`, so its spotlight is a full-width strip.
  - All of these are checked in the browser.
- **Conflict dialog:**
  - A draft conflict opens a modal by itself.
  - The runner ends the tour, as with any modal.
- **Labels:**
  - In es, pt and fr, "Save" and the device status share a word (Guardar/Guardado,
    Enregistrer/Enregistré). The copy says "on this device" and "on the server" explicitly.
  - Submit is "Envoyer" in fr (not "Soumettre") and "Enviar" in es and pt.
  - The View menu is Vista (es), Ver (pt) and Affichage (fr).

## UI/UX

- **Improve:** legacy explains nothing. The guide covers its confusing parts: Unaccounted Quantity,

- **Drop:** nothing.
- **Phone:** the same steps against the card layout. The lines steps cover the card list.

## Decisions

- **One step on the list:** the rest of the page is the shared picker and per-row controls.
- **Two steps on one lines anchor:** the columns repeat per card, so they cannot be anchored. Two steps
  keep each tooltip short.
- **No dialog steps:** opening dialogs ends a tour by design, and each dialog explains itself.
- **Delete and Save/Submit as separate steps:** they sit at opposite ends of the footer.
- **Lines anchor kept over empty states:** steps 7 and 8 are worded to read true without lines, so the
  tour still names Current Stock and Reasons on a draft where nothing is listed yet.
- **Submit explained in the actions step:** the confusing part of legacy is why Submit fails, so step 10
  names every condition.
