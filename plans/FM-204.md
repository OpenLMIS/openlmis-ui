(React Joyride, isolated module)

Stacked on FM-203 (PR #151).

## Scope

- **In:**
  - a tour for the Physical Inventory program list;
  - a tour for the draft editor;
  - `data-tour` anchors on both screens;
  - one runner fix for every tour: a step with `spotlight` scrolls to its own target, not the spotlit
    element, which otherwise pushed the target under the app header.
  - the runner waits up to 30 s (was 20 s) for a loading page: the draft took 18 to 25 s on the test
    server.
- **Out:**
  - anchors in shared components: the stock program picker (shared with Issue, Receive, Adjustments
    and Unpack), `data-table/` and the workspace footer;
  - per-row parts: status badges, Start/Continue, per-line inputs and Reasons buttons;
  - the dialogs (Add Products, Reasons, Occurred Date, Print), which explain themselves;
  - barcode scanning, which sits behind a flag.

## Summary

Physical Inventory gets a Show Guide button on both screens.

The program list guide explains, in one step, that each program at the home facility shows whether a
count is under way:
- Start begins a new count;
- Continue opens the draft;
- offline, only drafts saved on this device open.

The draft guide walks through:
- the progress bar;
- Filters (Keywords and Include Inactive Items);
- the saved-on-this-device status, and how it differs from Save;
- Packs/Doses and View;
- Add Product;
- the lines in two steps:
  - enter Current Stock, with VVM where it applies;
  - add Reasons for any difference until Unaccounted Quantity is 0;
- Delete;
- Save and Submit, with the date and signature asked on Submit.

Legacy has the same screens and explains nothing. Its users stumble on these, and the guide covers each:
- Unaccounted Quantity's sign;
- why Submit fails;
- the difference between autosave and Save.

### Rules

- **No anchors on the loading toolbar:**
  - `InventoryToolbar` also renders, disabled, in the busy pending body.
  - It sets its anchors only when not `disabled`, so a load longer than the runner's 30 s wait gives
    up instead of playing a partial tour on placeholders that are then replaced under an open step.
- **Lines steps hang from Add Product:** the grid starts right under the toolbar and fills the screen,
  so a `top` tooltip ran off the top at 1440. `center` fits but Joyride then drops the spotlight. The
  tooltip targets Add Product and the spotlight stays on the lines (`spotlight`, as Home's sections).
  Joyride scrolls to the spotlit element by default, which hid Add Product under the header on phones
  and long lists, so the runner sets `scrollTarget` to the step's own target.
- **Lines anchor outside the grid internals:**
  - Wrap the `filtered.length ? grid : empty` ternary, so no-matches still has the anchor.
  - The editor's focus query (`region.querySelector`) still finds the table or list.
- **Footer anchors inside the feature:**
  - `WorkspaceFooterPortal` is shared, so the anchors go on the feature's own Delete `Button` and
    Save/Submit div.
  - The Delete anchor passes through `ActionTooltip`'s render.
- **States with no anchors:**
  - These show only an empty state: no draft, a missing device copy offline, no view right, and a load error.
  - The runner gives up after 5 s, as on other screens.
- **Disabled buttons:**
  - Delete and Save are disabled offline. Submit stays enabled and explains that it needs a connection.
  - Save and Submit stay disabled while the eligible products load (`writeDisabled`,
    `eligibleFetching`); that load is not `aria-busy`, so the tour can start on them disabled.
  - The steps still name them.
- **Late changes under an open step:**
  - The list's per-row statuses load as plain skeletons, not `aria-busy`, so rows can read Start, then
    Continue.
  - The VVM column can appear once eligible products load, widening the table.
  - The draft status can grow to a failure sentence with Retry.
  - At narrow widths the status div is `basis-full`, so its spotlight is a full-width strip.
  - All of these are checked in the browser.
- **Conflict dialog:**
  - A draft conflict opens a modal by itself.
  - The runner ends the tour, as with any modal.
- **Labels:**
  - In es, pt and fr, "Save" and the device status share a word (Guardar/Guardado,
    Enregistrer/Enregistré). The copy says "on this device" and "on the server" explicitly.
  - Submit is "Envoyer" in fr (not "Soumettre") and "Enviar" in es and pt.
  - The View menu is Vista (es), Ver (pt) and Affichage (fr).

## UI/UX

- **Improve:** legacy explains nothing. The guide covers its confusing parts: Unaccounted Quantity,
  reasons, autosave against Save, and what Submit asks for.
- **Drop:** nothing.
- **Phone:** the same steps against the card layout. The lines steps cover the card list.
- **Arabic:** placements mirror through the module.

## Decisions

- **One step on the list:** the rest of the page is the shared picker and per-row controls.
- **Two steps on one lines anchor:** the columns repeat per card, so they cannot be anchored. Two steps
  keep each tooltip short.
- **No dialog steps:** opening dialogs ends a tour by design, and each dialog explains itself.
- **Delete and Save/Submit as separate steps:** they sit at opposite ends of the footer.
- **Lines anchor kept over empty states:** steps 7 and 8 are worded to read true without lines, so the
  tour still names Current Stock and Reasons on a draft where nothing is listed yet.
- **Submit explained in the actions step:** the confusing part of legacy is why Submit fails, so step 10
  names every condition.
