# FM-202: Onboarding Tour: Stock On Hand And Stock Card

https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-202 · Parent: [FM-199] Guided onboarding tours for new users
(React Joyride, isolated module)

Stacked on FM-201 (PR #149), which is stacked on FM-200 (PR #148).

## Scope

- **In:**
  - a tour for Stock On Hand and one for Stock Card;
  - `data-tour` anchors on both screens.
- **Out:**
  - other onboarding module changes; these land here, all found while testing these screens and
    applying to every tour:
    - the runner waits for the page body to stop loading (up to 20 s) before counting steps, and
      gives up only when a loaded page has no anchors for 5 s;
    - Show Guide clicked while the next page loads waits for that page and plays its guide;
    - the button shows a spinner and `aria-busy` while the guide is getting ready (the top bar is
      ignored when checking for loading, since the button itself is busy);
  - anchors in shared components (the facility and program picker, breadcrumbs);
  - per-row View buttons, which repeat.

## Summary

Stock On Hand and the Stock Card (legacy's bin card) get a Show Guide button.

The Stock On Hand guide walks through:
- choosing a facility and program, then Search;
- finding products and filtering them;
- the Packs/Doses switch, View and Print;
- how the results read: products show totals and lots sit below them, each with View to open
  its Stock Card.

The Stock Card guide walks through:
- Print and the product summary;
- the Packs/Doses switch and View;
- the line history, with links to the transactions behind it.

Before Search, the Stock On Hand guide shows only what is on screen.

## Agent Brief

### Steps: Stock On Hand (`tours/stock-on-hand.tour.ts`)

Route id `/(protected)/_protected/stock-management/stock-on-hand`.

| # | Anchor | Placed on | Before Search |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `stock-on-hand-picker` | new plain div around `FacilityProgramSelector` in the route | shown |
| 2 | `stock-on-hand-search` | Product Name div, `stock-on-hand-toolbar.tsx` | shown |
| 3 | `stock-on-hand-filters` | Filters div, `stock-on-hand-toolbar.tsx` | shown |
| 4 | `stock-on-hand-unit` | unit toggle div, `stock-on-hand-toolbar.tsx` | left out |
| 5 | `stock-on-hand-view` | View div, `stock-on-hand-toolbar.tsx` | left out |
| 6 | `stock-on-hand-print` | Print div, `stock-on-hand-toolbar.tsx` | left out |
| 7 | `stock-on-hand-results` | new `flex flex-col gap-4` div around the results `QueryBoundary` in the route, so the notice above the list keeps its gap | left out |

Content intent:
1. Choose My Facility or Supervised Facility, then the program (and, for Supervised, the facility),
   and press Search.
2. Find a product by name.
3. Filters hold Product Code, Lot Code and Include Inactive Items.
4. Show quantities in packs or doses.
5. Choose the columns or card details to show.
6. Download a PDF of all stock at the facility for the program, whatever the filters.
7. Neutral: your stock appears here. Products show their total with lots below, the chevron
   collapses a product's lots, View (an eye icon on a phone) opens a lot's Stock Card, and the pager moves between pages.
   It must read true over the skeleton, empty states and notices the wrapper also covers.

### Steps: Stock Card (`tours/stock-card.tour.ts`)

| # | Anchor | Placed on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `stock-card-print` | Print `Button` in the header actions, directly: `WorkspaceActions` stretches its direct children with `*:flex-1`, so a wrapper would take the stretch and the button would stop filling the row on phones |

| 4 | `stock-card-view` | View div in the route toolbar |
| 5 | `stock-card-lines` | new plain div around `StockCardLines` only; the offline summary-only card is left out |

Content intent:
1. Print the bin card as a PDF (the English page calls it Bin Card).
2. The product with its code and pack size, facility, program, stock on hand, and lot and expiry
   when the card has a lot.
3. Show quantities in packs or doses.
4. Choose the columns or card details to show; Signature starts hidden, and Reversing and Reversed
   By show only on cards with reversals.
5. Each line is a stock change with the balance after it; entries under Document Number link to
   Transaction History, and the pager moves between pages.

Print sits in the page header above the summary, so it comes first and the tour runs top to bottom.

Placements: results and lines use `top`; the rest use `bottom`.

### Rules

- **No anchor inside the shared picker:**
  - The picker's Search button lives in a component shared by four screens. Step 1 covers the whole
    picker and names Search, as FM-200 did.
- **No View-per-row anchor:**
  - `querySelector` would pick the first row, and rows repeat, collapse and go stale.
  - Step 7 explains View instead.
- **No anchor on the Stock Card toolbar itself:**
  - It is empty and zero-sized when there is no unit toggle and no View.
- **Loading:**
  - The runner fixes its steps once. It now waits until the page body has no `aria-busy` skeleton
    (at most 5 s) before counting them, so a guide started during a reload keeps every step.
    Anchors on skeletons were tried first and dropped in review: the real content replaces them
    under an open step, which then stays at the screen corner.
  - The Stock Card route blocks behind its pending view. The launcher waits while the router is
    pending, then the runner waits while the page body is busy, up to 20 s in all.

## UI/UX

- **Improve:** legacy explains nothing. The guide names the order of work (choose, Search, read
  totals, open a card) and where Packs/Doses, View and Print are.
- **Drop:** nothing.
- **Phone:** the same steps against the card layout; the results step covers the card list.
- **Arabic:** placements mirror through the module.

## Decisions

- **Search inside the picker step:** shared components stay anchor-free.
- **Wait for loading in the runner, not anchors on skeletons:** both reviews of the skeleton anchors
  found the open step stranded when the real page replaced them.
- **Opening a Stock Card is explained in the results step:** there is no separate step, because View
  repeats per lot and may not be on screen.
- **Before Search, show only the parts on screen:** there is no step for the empty state, whose
  text would not match a results description.
