# FM-202: Onboarding Tour: Stock On Hand And Stock Card

https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-202 · Parent: [FM-199] Guided onboarding tours for new users
(React Joyride, isolated module)

Stacked on FM-201 (PR #149), which is stacked on FM-200 (PR #148).

## Scope

- **In:**
  - a tour for Stock On Hand and one for Stock Card;
  - `data-tour` anchors on both screens.
- **Out:**
  - changes to the onboarding module;
  - anchors in shared components (the facility and program picker, breadcrumbs);
  - per-row View buttons, which repeat.

## Summary

Stock On Hand and the Stock Card (legacy's bin card) get a Show Guide button.

The Stock On Hand guide walks through:
- choosing a facility and program, then Search;
- finding products and filtering them;
- the Packs/Doses switch, View and Print;
- how the results read: products show totals and lots sit below them, each with View to open
  its Stock Card.

The Stock Card guide walks through:
- the product summary and Print;
- the Packs/Doses switch and View;
- the line history, with links to the transactions behind it.

Before Search, the Stock On Hand guide shows only what is on screen.

Content intent:

2. Print the Stock Card as a PDF.
3. Show quantities in packs or doses.
4. Choose the columns or card details to show.
5. Each line is one stock event with the balance after it; document numbers link to Transaction
   History, and the pager moves between pages.

Print comes second so the tour runs top to bottom without scrolling back up.

Placements: results and lines use `top`; the rest use `bottom`.

### Rules

- **No anchor inside the shared picker:**
  - The picker's Search button lives in a component shared by four screens. Step 1 covers the whole
    picker and names Search, as FM-200 did.
- **No View-per-row anchor:**
  - `querySelector` would pick the first row, and rows repeat, collapse and go stale.
  - Step 7 explains View instead.
- **No anchor on the Stock Card toolbar itself:**
  - It is empty and zero-sized when there is no unit toggle and no View.
- **Loading:**
  - The runner fixes its steps once, from what is on screen. The Stock On Hand skeleton already
    renders the real toolbar, so the picker and results anchors also go on their skeleton wrappers;
    otherwise a guide started during a reload loses steps 1 and 7. These skeletons have size, so
    FM-200's rule against anchoring zero-size skeletons does not apply.
  - The Stock Card route blocks behind its pending view, which has no anchors. The launcher
    resolves the card route meanwhile and the runner waits up to 5 s for the first anchor. A
    slower load ends the guide silently; that is a known limit.

## UI/UX

- **Improve:** legacy explains nothing. The guide names the order of work (choose, Search, read
  totals, open a card) and where Packs/Doses, View and Print are.
- **Drop:** nothing.
- **Phone:** the same steps against the card layout; the results step covers the card list.
- **Arabic:** placements mirror through the module.

## Decisions

- **Search inside the picker step:** shared components stay anchor-free.
- **Anchors on two skeletons:** a guide started while Stock On Hand reloads still plays every step
  (plan review).
- **Opening a Stock Card is explained in the results step:** there is no separate step, because View
  repeats per lot and may not be on screen.
- **Before Search, show only the parts on screen:** there is no step for the empty state, whose
  text would not match a results description.
