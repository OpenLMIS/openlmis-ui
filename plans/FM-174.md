# FM-174: Theme Selection

https://openlmis.atlassian.net/browse/FM-174 · Parent: FM-170 Global app configuration
(branding, theme & feature flags)

## Scope

This PR adds the **Theme** tab to System Settings and five more colour presets beside
today's blue. The administrator picks a preset and the default appearance (light, dark or
follow the system). It builds on FM-172 (the preset mechanism and boot) and FM-173 (the
page, its footer, rights and save pattern).

It does not change status colours, the user's own light/dark switch, or any component's
shape. It adds no free colour picker.

## Summary

The administrator chooses one of six colour themes, each shown as a small swatch, and
whether the app opens light, dark or following the device. A preview shows buttons, a badge
and a chart in both light and dark, so the choice is made with open eyes.

Every theme is checked automatically for readable contrast in both modes, and the success,
warning and error colours never change, so status always reads the same. A user who has
already chosen light or dark for themselves keeps their choice; the default is for everyone
else. Legacy has one fixed blue and no dark mode.

| i18n, RTL, no console errors | New keys in every locale | `tsc`; browser in Arabic |

## Legacy Today

One compiled SCSS palette (`$brand-primary: #49baeb`, login button `rgb(58,149,188)`), no
dark mode, and country forks override `_variables.scss` and rebuild. Nothing to port; the
new app already has light and dark.

### Presets

`blue` (default, today's hue 240), `teal`, `green`, `indigo`, `purple`, `slate`. No red,
orange or amber: they would read as the destructive and warning status colours.

Each preset sets, for light and dark: `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--brand`,
`--sidebar-primary`, `--sidebar-primary-foreground`, `--chart-1` to `--chart-5`. The rings
stay shared in `globals.css`. Everything else in `globals.css` (surfaces, `success`,
`warning`, `destructive`, `info`, borders) is shared by every preset.

Chart ramps keep the blue ramp's lightness steps and change the hue, with chroma clamped to
the sRGB gamut, so every ramp has the steps and contrast the blue one was checked for.

### Rules

- Contrast, per preset and mode: `--primary-foreground` on `--primary` at least 4.5:1;
  `--primary` on `--background`, the sidebar's active colour and each chart step on `--card`
  within 10% of blue's own; lightness strictly monotonic across the five
  steps; every token inside the sRGB gamut.
- Unknown preset from the server: `blue` (FM-172).
- The admin's own session applies the new preset and default appearance on save, through
  the store; a user who picked Light or Dark keeps it.
- The preview sets the preset's variables inline on its own wrapper, and renders a second
  copy inside a `dark` wrapper, so it never touches the live page before Save.

### Risks and Edge Cases

- Moving tokens out of `globals.css` must leave the default look pixel-identical; FM-172
  does the move, this PR only adds presets.
- A future `shadcn` preset switch rewrites `globals.css`; the tokens a preset owns live in
  `theme-presets.ts`, so they survive, but AGENTS.md's warning about re-applying variants
  still holds.

## UI/UX

- **Preset picker:** a grid of cards, each with the name and a swatch strip (primary plus
  the five chart steps), selected state from `ChoiceCard`. Keyboard: radio group arrows.
- **Appearance:** three cards, Light, Dark, System, with a one-line description for System.
- **Preview:** side by side light and dark panels, stacked on a narrow page, showing a
  primary and outline button, a badge and a small bar chart. It uses the real components.
- **Footer and reset:** as on Branding.
- **Improve:** legacy has one palette and no dark mode; here the choice is safe by
  construction.

## Decisions

1. **Six fixed presets, no colour picker**, as the ticket asks; readability is guaranteed by
   a test, not by review.
2. **No red, orange or amber presets**, so the primary colour never looks like a status.
   Reversed as built; see below.
3. **Status colours are outside presets**, so success, warning and error mean the same in
   every deployment.
4. **Ramps derived from blue by hue**, so the checked steps carry over instead of hand-tuning
   six ramps.

5. **The user's switch gains a way back to the default.** It only toggles light and dark
   today, so a user who has toggled could never return to the deployment's default. The
   switch becomes a small menu (Light, Dark, Default), where Default clears the stored
   choice.

## As Built

- `teal` (195), `green` (150), `indigo` (275), `purple` (295) and `slate` (255, chroma x0.3) are
  derived from `blue` in `src/lib/theme-presets.ts`: same lightness per token, new hue, chroma
  clamped into sRGB. Purple sits at 295 because 305 read dimmer than blue in dark mode.
- The contrast test, measured against today's blue: button text on `primary` must be at least
  4.5:1 in both modes; every other pair (primary on the background, the sidebar's active colour,
  each chart step on a card) must be within 10% of blue's own. Blue already sits below 4.5:1 for
  the sidebar's active colour in light mode (3.8:1) and for `primary` on the dark background
  (2.6:1), so the rule is "no worse than today", not an absolute bar for those.
- Blue is exempt from the gamut check: it is today's palette, a few of its tokens sit just
  outside sRGB and the browser maps them, so it stays exactly as it was.
- The preview and swatches set the preset's CSS variables with an inline object of custom
  properties only, which is what shadcn/lint allows; a generated `<style>` is refused.
- The preview is `inert` and `aria-hidden`, so its sample Save and Cancel are not buttons to a
  screen reader or the keyboard.
- The user's switch is a menu: Light, Dark, Use Default. Use Default forgets the stored choice.
- The form holds only valid choices, so it has no zod schema; its fields are `preset` and
  `defaultAppearance`, the stored names.
- Reset To Defaults stays focusable while disabled, so focus is not lost after confirming. It is
  disabled whenever blue following the device is in use, stored or not.
- `RadioGroup` gains `columns="fill"`: one column, two from `@md/main`, three from `@2xl/main`.
- The preview panels carry `light` and `dark` classes, and the `dark` variant is
  `&:is(.dark *):not(.light, .light *)`, so the light panel stays light while the app is dark.
- Eighteen presets: blue, sapphire, indigo, purple, fuchsia, pink, rose, red, orange, amber,
  olive, green, emerald, teal, cyan, brown, slate and graphite. Red, orange and amber came in at
  the product owner's request, reversing Decision 2; status meaning still never rests on colour
  alone, since it always carries an icon and a label. Pink and rose run at 0.7 chroma, red at
  0.8, fuchsia at 0.75, olive at 0.7 and brown at 0.45 to meet the contrast rules in dark mode;
  graphite has none. They are tiles, the swatch above the name, six to a row on a wide page.
- The default appearance is one row of three cards, Light, Dark and Device, each with its icon
  and a short description.
- The preview shows buttons, a badge, switches, a progress bar and a chart across the full width.

## Open Questions

- Today's blue sits below WCAG AA for `primary` on the dark background (2.6:1) and the sidebar's
  active colour in light mode (3.8:1), so "no worse than blue" is the bar the presets meet.
  Raising those two tokens changes every deployment's current look; the ticket owner decides.
